# Emirates Inbound Illustration — Per-City Story Elements

## Convention

Every illustration shows the same Emirates crew member arriving **home to Dubai (DXB)** from one of the 124 network destinations. The base look never changes — the Emirates uniform, the red pillbox hat with white veil, and her **black rolling suitcase**. To signal *where she just flew in from*, we add two subtle, tasteful cues:

1. **STICKER / CHARM** — one small, iconic graphic on the black rolling suitcase (a flat luggage-sticker decal), optionally paired with a tiny dangling charm on the telescopic handle. Understated, not a sticker-bombed case: one clear landmark or motif per city so it reads at a glance without shouting.
2. **ACCESSORY** *(optional)* — a single, elegant on-body swap that evokes the city (a delicate bracelet, a tucked flower, a hat swap, a scarf). Only ONE item changes, and only where it stays refined and respectful. Where no accessory works gracefully, the field is `-` and the suitcase sticker carries the story alone.

Design rules: iconic over literal, elegant over loud, cultural respect always. One city read = one sticker + at most one accessory. Each row keyed by IATA (London LGW/LHR/STN, Tokyo HND/NRT, and Karachi KHI day/evening share their city motif).

Total destinations: 124
